Bible text credits
All Names Edition (v2.15): plain modern English from the World English Bible (WEB, public domain; ebible.org eng-web) for all 66 books and for the Apocrypha (1–2 Esdras, Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach with its Prologue, Baruch, Letter of Jeremiah, Prayer of Manasseh, Psalm 151, Prayer of Azariah, Susanna, Bel and the Dragon, 1–4 Maccabees). Every name of God, the Lord and Jesus is followed by the original names in parentheses. Verse numbers follow the KJV (Romans 16:25–27; four NT verses that WEB leaves out are kept from the KJV-based text).
Additions to Esther: WEB for 10:4–11:1 and 13:8–14:19; the rest from the KJV 1611 Apocrypha (modernized), because WEB prints these additions inside Greek Esther with different verse divisions. A few Sirach verses absent from WEB are kept from the KJV-based text.
Other books, public-domain English, lightly modernized (thee/thou/ye, -eth, “it came to pass”, etc.): 1 Enoch and Jubilees (R. H. Charles), 2 Enoch, Testaments of the Twelve Patriarchs, 4 Baruch, Ascension of Isaiah, Joseph and Asenath, 1–2 Clement, the Didache, Shepherd of Hermas, Book of Jasher (1840), Gospel of Thomas (Mark M. Mattison, public domain), Questions of Bartholomew (M. R. James, 1924).
Dead Sea Scrolls credits
Damascus Document (Cairo Genizah Texts A & B; also called “Fragments of a Zadokite Work”): English translation by R. H. Charles, in The Apocrypha and Pseudepigrapha of the Old Testament in English, vol. 2 (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1913), pp. 799–834. License: public domain (published 1913; translator died 1931). Source scan: archive.org/details/apocryphapseudep02charuoft (marked NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT). Text taken from the scan’s OCR and proofread; chapters and verses follow Charles. Archaic English lightly modernized as in the rest of the app; in JSV, God→Yahuah. “[...]” marks gaps in the manuscript. The intro note is an app note, not part of the translation.
Other Qumran writings (Community Rule, War Scroll, Thanksgiving Hymns, Temple Scroll, Habakkuk Pesher, Genesis Apocryphon, Rule of the Congregation, Rule of Blessings, Copper Scroll, Songs of the Sabbath Sacrifice) are not included because no public-domain or openly licensed (CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-SA) English translation could be found; all were discovered after 1947 and the available translations are still under copyright.
Definitions: WordNet • Princeton University • Strong's Concordance • Easton's, Smith's & Hitchcock's Bible dictionaries (public domain)
